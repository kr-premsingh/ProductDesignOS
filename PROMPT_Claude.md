# ProductDesignOS Product Brief

ProductDesignOS is a design operating system for individuality-first design.

## Tone

Premium, confident, curious, slightly playful, and human-first.

## Visual Direction

Charcoal foundation, high contrast, soft neon cyan and magenta accents, restrained glass panels, crisp motion, generous whitespace, and minimal copy.

## Core Flows

- Landing to early access: hero, CTA, modal, role, preference, thank-you state
- Inspire to remix: tile overlay, prefilled AI prompt, variant generation, save action
- Profile creation: role, tagline, hero/gallery, publish
- Provider onboarding: verification, portfolio, categories, capabilities, approval

## Copy

- Hero: Templates are dead. Design your signature.
- Subtag: AI and human craft for designs that feel like you.
- CTAs: Join Early Access, Remix with AI, Become a Provider
- Empty Inspire: We are curating brilliance. Come back soon or add your first design.
- AI Progress: Crafting your variants. This takes a few seconds.

## AI Rules

- Avoid generating copyrighted logos or trademarked characters
- Ask for confirmation before derivative brand work
- Return three distinct variants with short rationales and color guidance
